# Emerald Rupture damage calc

A copy of [Dynamic-Calc](https://github.com/hzla/Dynamic-Calc) (by hzla) set up for **Emerald Rupture 2.9.1**.

- Normal mode: `/normal.html`
- Hard mode: `/hard.html`
- Rewind mode (bosses in reverse order): `/rewind.html` and `/rewind-hard.html`
- Documentation site (every trainer, Pokédex, moves, encounters, TMs, items, with Calc links): `/docs/`
- Boss Prep (`/docs/#prep`): pick the next gym, League, villain or rival fight (tabs, in story order), see every Pokémon you could have by then, who wins each 1-on-1 at the level cap, and new TMs/items nearby; star the ones you caught, or read your .sav to check your real team (levels, IVs, moves), jump to your next boss and, in a Nuzlocke, see what you can still catch
- Nuzlocke tracker: `/nuzlocke/`

Click **Read .sav**, pick your `.sav` file, and your party and all 14 PC boxes load into the calc.
Trainer teams (with exact levels, moves, abilities, items and IVs), Pokémon stats/types/abilities/learnsets and move data
all come from the game's own source, so they match the ROM.

The data files (`backups/rupture*.js`, `js/save_constants/rupture.js`) are generated by
`tools/gen5/calc_export.py` in the game repo, and the docs site data (`docs/data/*.json`) by `tools/gen5/site_export.py` (Boss Prep: `prep-*.json` from `tools/gen5/balance_check.py --site`); the save reader is `js/savereader_rupture.js`.

Notes: the calc engine is Gen 8 (the newest it has), so a few Gen 9-only move and ability effects aren't modelled.

A docs link like `index.html?data=rupture&...&opp=<set name>` opens the calc with that trainer Pokémon already loaded (`js/rupture_links.js`).
